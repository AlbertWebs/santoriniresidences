## THE PROJECT
### Where Architecture Meets Everyday Life

Santorini is the flagship development of LOVE HOMES — the brand's first landmark project in Nairobi.

Inspired by the relaxed character of Santorini, Greece, the development brings together contemporary architecture, resort-style amenities, and thoughtfully planned residences within the heart of Westlands.

It's designed for people who want more from home — more comfort, more convenience, more lifestyle, and a stronger sense of place.

---

## THE LANDMARK
### Designed to Be Recognised

Santorini is conceived as an architectural landmark within Westlands.

Its twin-tower façade moves away from the conventional straight residential block, introducing flowing curves, expansive glazing, and sculptural aluminium elements. The building's silhouette stands out by day; its architectural lighting gives it a distinct identity after dark.

**A Building With a Signature**
The architectural language draws on the movement of a sail and the flowing curves associated with Santorini itself — the result is a residential landmark built to stand apart from conventional apartment architecture.

## THE 8 UNIQUE FEATURES

*(Design note: present as eight large cards, each with an image/icon, headline, and short description — this should be one of the most visually prominent sections of the site.)*

**01. Landmark Architecture — Beautiful by Design. Functional by Purpose.**
Santorini is designed as a distinctive architectural landmark in Westlands, combining sculptural curves, expansive glazing, and contemporary forms to create a recognisable presence on the Nairobi skyline. The architecture isn't purely aesthetic — every element is shaped around the way residents live, work, relax, and experience the city.

**02. Signature Glass Façade & Fluorocarbon Aluminium Panels — A Façade Designed to Stand Out.**
The building's signature façade pairs expansive glass surfaces with sculpted fluorocarbon aluminium panels, creating an identity that shifts with the light throughout the day. The combination delivers a refined, high-end exterior while supporting durability, weather resistance, and thermal and acoustic comfort — a façade that's both visually distinctive and purposefully engineered.

**03. A True Mixed-Use Development — More Than a Residence. A Complete Urban Destination.**
Santorini brings residential living, retail, dining, wellness, and leisure together within one integrated development. Residents can access essential services and lifestyle amenities without leaving the building — retail, dining, wellness, recreation, and residence, all under one address.

**04. A Full-Service Supermarket with 6-Metre Ceilings — Everyday Convenience, Elevated.**
A dedicated, approximately 600 m² fully franchised supermarket brings everyday shopping closer to home. With a 6-metre ceiling height, it's designed to feel spacious, modern, and premium — closer to a destination retail experience than a conventional residential convenience store.

**05. Double-Height Dining, Sky Lounge & Elevated Social Spaces — Dine. Gather. Unwind.**
Santorini extends the residential experience into elevated social spaces, including a double-height restaurant and sky lounge for dining, entertaining, and relaxed city living — spaces where residents can host guests, share a meal, meet friends, or simply take in the view above the city. Hospitality-inspired design meets residential comfort.

**06. Approximately 600 m² Fully Equipped Fitness Centre — Wellness Built Into Everyday Life.**
A dedicated, approximately 600 m² fully equipped fitness centre gives residents a substantial wellness facility within the development, making it easier to maintain an active lifestyle without travelling across the city.

**07. Same-Floor Drainage System — Smarter Plumbing. Easier Long-Term Maintenance.**
Santorini incorporates a same-floor drainage system, keeping drainage services accessible within the relevant floor zone to simplify maintenance and reduce the disruption traditionally associated with plumbing works. It also supports better noise control around plumbing-related disturbance — a feature most residents may never notice, but one they'll appreciate over the lifetime of the property.

**08. High-End Management & Premium Resident Experience — A Premium Product for Premium Living.**
Santorini is supported by a high-end management approach focused on maintaining the quality, functionality, and overall experience of the property — from arrival and shared spaces to amenities, maintenance, and day-to-day operations. Built for people who value design, convenience, quality, and the experience of where they live.

**The Santorini Difference**
Distinctive architecture. Integrated retail. Elevated dining. Dedicated wellness. Thoughtful engineering. High-end management.

Santorini brings these elements together to create a premium mixed-use residential landmark in Westlands, Nairobi — a development designed not simply to provide a home, but a more complete way of living.
